The first four images are Discover song covers; the last two are input portraits from published performance demos. **These new briefs interpret visible imagery; they are not recovered source prompts and are not render-tested.** Click an image for its source. <a id="inputs"></a>

## Choose the input workflow before preparing assets

The four cover-based projects offer two ways to start:

- **Text only:** paste a shot prompt into either official H3 or MusicMaker. Images are references for your own planning, not uploads. Text alone does not ensure the same person across shots; begin with one portrait take connected by environment shots.
- **Image guided:** official H3 offers first-frame or image-reference modes. MusicMaker’s short-video form needs **both Start Frame and End Frame for each shot**. Use authorized images with closely related framing—for example, a modest crop of the same base image—while preserving the person, ratio and light. Avoid pairing unrelated front and rear views for your first test.

The image subjects listed below guide composition; prepare the number of files required by your chosen route. The two vocal projects instead require one portrait and a vocal excerpt. [Follow the upload steps](first-video.md).

## Neon singer teaser

<p align="center"><a href="https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-94/"><img src="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/discover_v2/neon_pulse.webp" alt="Singer holding a microphone in front of a neon ring — open the original brand example" width="560"></a></p>

**Neon Pulse**

Borrow the cover’s pink-blue ring and microphone for a teaser alternating stage views and singer close-ups.

**Bring**

Singer references prepared for your [chosen input route](#inputs), plus a 16-second musical phrase. Keep clothing, hair and microphone consistent.

[Source page](https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-94/)

**Shot and edit plan**

1. 0–4s: medium view of singer and complete ring.
2. 4–8s: microphone and hand detail, minimal movement.
3. 8–12s: head-and-shoulders view, ring behind the singer.
4. 12–16s: return to medium framing; add the title in the edit.

**Copy the main-shot prompt**

```text
An adult singer in a black jacket holds one microphone before a pink and blue
neon ring. Medium shot, very slow push-in, subtle lighting variation and a
small nod. Keep the outfit unchanged. No text, no second microphone.
```

Keep identity and lighting in every prompt; change the framing to hand detail, head-and-shoulders, then locked medium shot. For that final locked shot, also remove the slow push-in instruction. Use the vocal route if the singer must perform supplied words.

**Two tools:** [Official Hailuo H3](https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3) or [MusicMaker short-video tool](https://musicmaker.im/free-short-music-video-generator/). Generate separate short shots and trim each to four seconds for a 16-second draft; re-mark cuts for your actual song. Image workflows require your own authorized references and the chosen form’s required inputs.

**Check and fix:** Place the first face close-up on a musical emphasis. Reduce lighting changes if the ring deforms; return to a single shot if identity drifts.

## Piano by the window

<p align="center"><a href="https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-95/"><img src="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/discover_v2/echoes_of_you.webp" alt="Man seated at a piano beside a bright window — open the original brand example" width="560"></a></p>

**Echoes of You**

Use the cover’s piano, window light and flowers to build a quiet performance setting.

**Bring**

Piano-room references prepared for your [chosen input route](#inputs), plus 16 seconds of music. Start with atmosphere, not a claim of accurate piano performance.

[Source page](https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-95/)

**Shot and edit plan**

1. 0–4s: wide piano room beside the window.
2. 4–8s: flowers beside the piano, person out of frame.
3. 8–12s: side portrait with hands outside the frame.
4. 12–16s: light across the piano lid, space for the title.

**Copy the main-shot prompt**

```text
A bright room with an adult man seated at a piano by a window, flowers nearby,
cream and warm wood tones. Slowly approach the piano from a wide room view.
Curtains move gently; the person stays seated with hands outside the frame. No
text.
```

Generate the flowers, side portrait and light on the lid separately; repeat the room and palette. Do not request four cuts inside one prompt.

**Two tools:** [Official Hailuo H3](https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3) or [MusicMaker short-video tool](https://musicmaker.im/free-short-music-video-generator/). Generate separate short shots and trim each to four seconds for a 16-second draft; re-mark cuts for your actual song. Image workflows require your own authorized references and the chosen form’s required inputs.

**Check and fix:** Cut at phrase changes rather than every note. Replace malformed keys or fingers with flowers, lid or shoulder detail; actual playing technique needs separate verification.

## Open-road diary

<p align="center"><a href="https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-104/"><img src="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/discover_v2/the_open_road.webp" alt="A road through golden fields viewed from a car dashboard — open the original brand example" width="560"></a></p>

**The Open Road**

Use the dashboard, map and golden fields to suggest departure, travel and arrival.

**Bring**

Travel references prepared for your [chosen input route](#inputs), 16 seconds of music, consistent weather and car interior colors.

[Source page](https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-104/)

**Shot and edit plan**

1. 0–4s: map on the dashboard while parked.
2. 4–8s: view along the open road from inside.
3. 8–12s: golden fields passing a side window.
4. 12–16s: open destination landscape with an edited title.

**Copy the main-shot prompt**

```text
View from the front seat along a two-lane road through golden fields under a
clear blue sky. A folded map and sunglasses rest on the dashboard. Locked
interior camera, smooth forward travel, roadside scenery moving slowly
backward. The map stays still; no road splits, no text.
```

Make the map shot while parked. Change only viewing direction for the side window. Remove the car foreground for the destination, retaining weather. Add readable signage only in the editor.

**Two tools:** [Official Hailuo H3](https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3) or [MusicMaker short-video tool](https://musicmaker.im/free-short-music-video-generator/). Generate separate short shots and trim each to four seconds for a 16-second draft; re-mark cuts for your actual song. Image workflows require your own authorized references and the chosen form’s required inputs.

**Check and fix:** Cut from map to road to make departure clear. Slow the motion if the horizon wobbles or road melts. Generated geography is not travel information.

## Summer coast teaser

<p align="center"><a href="https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-96/"><img src="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/discover_v2/summer_high.webp" alt="Smiling woman beside a sunlit coastal road — open the original brand example" width="560"></a></p>

**Summer High**

Build on the smile, coastal road and yellow clothing, alternating person and landscape.

**Bring**

Adult portrait references prepared for your [chosen input route](#inputs), plus 16 seconds of music. Preserve clothing, hair and sunlight direction.

[Source page](https://musicmaker.im/detail/discover-v2-96/)

**Shot and edit plan**

1. 0–4s: medium portrait by the coastal path.
2. 4–8s: sea and palm leaves, no person.
3. 8–12s: close-up of the same person smiling.
4. 12–16s: wider view with a few steps, plus an edited title.

**Copy the main-shot prompt**

```text
An adult woman in a yellow-and-white top stands beside a sunny coastal path,
ocean and palms behind her. Locked medium shot, a light breeze in her hair, a
natural smile toward the camera. Keep face and clothing consistent. No turn,
no text.
```

Use the same reference for the close-up; generate scenery separately. If text-only shots change identity, keep one portrait take and connect it with landscapes.

**Two tools:** [Official Hailuo H3](https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3) or [MusicMaker short-video tool](https://musicmaker.im/free-short-music-video-generator/). Generate separate short shots and trim each to four seconds for a 16-second draft; re-mark cuts for your actual song. Image workflows require your own authorized references and the chosen form’s required inputs.

**Check and fix:** Place the smile at a chosen musical change. Remove head turns or walking if the face distorts. Add the final title in the editor instead of asking the model to draw it.

## Warm guitar performance

<p align="center"><a href="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/ai_music_video_generator/example/example1_video.mp4"><img src="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/ai_music_video_generator/example/example1_cover.webp" alt="Seated woman holding an electric guitar under warm stage lighting — open the original brand example" width="560"></a></p>

**Guitar performance**

Start from the official demo’s guitar portrait and warm stage. First aim for one stable vocal take.

**Bring**

Your own guitar portrait and 5–10 seconds of vocals. Face, guitar, arms and microphone should be visually separate.

[Source page](https://musicmaker.im/ai-music-video-generator/)

**Shot and edit plan**

1. Opening: leave a short lead-in before the first syllable.
2. Middle: locked medium shot, singing first, minimal hand motion.
3. Ending: finish the complete phrase and leave a short hold; do not force 16 seconds.

**Copy the main-shot prompt**

```text
Use the supplied portrait for the singer and the uploaded audio for
performance timing. Warm small stage, seated singer holding an electric
guitar, locked medium shot. Natural singing, slight body movement, minimal
hand action. Preserve guitar shape, arm positions and microphone. No cut, no
text.
```

Use either vocal route in tutorial section 6 with both image and audio. Text alone in the short-scene form is not a replacement for vocal timing input.

**Two tools:** [Official Hailuo H3](https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3) with audio references, or [MusicMaker’s vocal tool](https://musicmaker.im/ai-music-video-generator/). The brand demo does not identify its model; these are workflow alternatives, not a same-model benchmark.

**Check and fix:** Check the mouth first, then guitar neck, strings and fingers. If playing motion fails, crop or regenerate a vocal close-up. Subtle movement is not proof of accurate playing.

## One-phrase vocal close-up

<p align="center"><a href="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/ai_music_video_generator/example/example2_video.mp4"><img src="https://cdn.musicmaker.im/musicmaker/ai_music_video_generator/example/example2_cover.webp" alt="Smiling singer at a gold microphone under pink stage lights — open the original brand example" width="560"></a></p>

**Emotional vocal**

Use the demo’s gold microphone, pink-blue stage and facial expression to focus on one phrase.

**Bring**

Your own clear half-body portrait and a complete 5–10-second vocal phrase. Keep the microphone below the mouth.

[Source page](https://musicmaker.im/ai-music-video-generator/)

**Shot and edit plan**

1. Opening: frontal medium close-up with a natural lead-in.
2. Middle: a small nod or expression change, no complex gesture.
3. Ending: hold the expression after the phrase before an audio fade.

**Copy the main-shot prompt**

```text
Use the uploaded portrait for appearance and the supplied audio for
performance timing. An adult singer stands behind a gold microphone under
pink-blue stage light. Head-and-shoulders framing, visible mouth, small nod,
natural expression. Stable camera. Preserve clothing, teeth and microphone. No
text.
```

Choose official H3 or the MusicMaker vocal workflow in section 6. Lock the first test’s camera; add a slow push-in only after identity remains stable.

**Two tools:** [Official Hailuo H3](https://hailuoai.video/tools/minimax-h3) with audio references, or [MusicMaker’s vocal tool](https://musicmaker.im/ai-music-video-generator/). The brand demo does not identify its model; these are workflow alternatives, not a same-model benchmark.

**Check and fix:** Inspect consonant attacks, held notes and the phrase ending. Shorten the excerpt if timing drifts; do not hide it behind rapid cuts.
