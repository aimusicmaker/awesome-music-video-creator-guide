## Pink studio duet

[![Two dancers on a pink studio set — @Just_sharon7](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2083422025452765184/img/ZFIIYS0hQ9OI5TpK.jpg)](https://x.com/Just_sharon7/status/2083422886686031982)

**[@Just_sharon7](https://x.com/Just_sharon7/status/2083422886686031982) · Seedance 2.5** · creator-reported model<br>
[☞ Original post, video + prompt](https://x.com/Just_sharon7/status/2083422886686031982) · Text prompt in the post.

**What to borrow:** Assign each performer a fixed position, then alternate wide choreography and solo close-ups.

Short source excerpt: “Vibrant hot pink, electric blue, and silver color palette.”

**Original practice prompt (untested):**

```text
Two adult dancers in an amber rehearsal studio. Keep the same outfits and left/right positions. Start wide, move to one dancer’s shoulder-level close-up, then return to the original wide framing for a shared final pose. Use one short licensed instrumental excerpt; align the last pose to a marked beat in the edit.
```

**Check first:** Count two performers in every wide shot; compare hands and foot contact across cuts.

## Reference-led singing with H3

[![A performer lying on the ground in the opening of TechHalla’s music clip — @techhalla](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2086903458251112448/img/jSPsz35xEe3ZFz47.jpg)](https://x.com/techhalla/status/2086915118307119269)

**[@techhalla](https://x.com/techhalla/status/2086915118307119269) · MiniMax H3 + Seedream 5 Pro** · creator-reported model<br>
[☞ Original post, video + prompt](https://x.com/techhalla/status/2086915118307119269) · Prompts are screenshots in the reply thread; open the thread, not only the lead post.

Thread reading mirror: [Thread Reader](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/2086915118307119269.html) · [Open the author’s timeline-prompt screenshot](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HPY1eQ_WoAAbakz.png)

**What to borrow:** Build character and location references before dividing the performance along the audio.

Short source excerpt: “I used 5 images + the full 15s of audio”

**Original practice prompt (untested):**

```text
Use one authorized singer portrait and one quiet rehearsal-room reference. For an eight-second licensed vocal excerpt, hold a medium frontal shot for the first phrase. Move gently closer during the held note, keeping the mouth visible. End on a relaxed breath. Preserve the supplied soundtrack and inspect the mouth timing before adding more shots.
```

**Check first:** Check that your chosen interface accepts audio references; a text-only route cannot supply these inputs.

Use an original dry-drum instrumental and make the three cuts in your editor.

## Shape changes for a visualizer

[![White concentric rings on a black background — @oggii_0](https://pbs.twimg.com/ext_tw_video_thumb/2041392519330840576/pu/img/gJnakn0aUsx_Pqnd.jpg)](https://x.com/oggii_0/status/2041392542659584302)

**[@oggii_0](https://x.com/oggii_0/status/2041392542659584302) · Seedance 2.0** · creator-reported model<br>
[☞ Original post, video + prompt](https://x.com/oggii_0/status/2041392542659584302) · Text prompt in the post; an adjacent motion-design reference.

**What to borrow:** Study one centered shape evolving over time. This source is motion design, not a music video.

Short source excerpt: “All motion occurs within the subject.”

**Original practice prompt (untested):**

```text
A single coral-colored paper ring hangs against a deep navy background. The locked camera watches the ring bend slowly into a ribbon and return to its opening shape. Keep the background still and the edges clean. No letters or logo. Add your own instrumental in the editor and adjust the transformation to one chosen musical phrase.
```

**Check first:** Returning to the starting shape does not guarantee a seamless loop; compare the first and final frames.

## A train journey into space

[![A traveler waiting on a dim station platform — @Strength04_X](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2098289904409362432/img/-LuU_JZblovIR9HB.jpg)](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2098290630179057858)

**[@Strength04_X](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2098290630179057858) · Seedance 2.5** · creator-reported model<br>
[☞ Original post, video + prompt](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2098290630179057858) · Text prompt in the post; it requires a character reference image.

**What to borrow:** Borrow departure, transformation and reveal for a narrative music video. The source is a fantasy short.

Short source excerpt: “Keep her face, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions and visual details unchanged”

**Original practice prompt (untested):**

```text
One adult traveler in a rust-colored coat boards a quiet train at dusk. Show the doorway crossing, then a medium shot inside the same carriage. Outside, city lights gradually give way to a calm fictional constellation. End on the traveler looking through the window. Preserve the coat and carriage. Edit the reveal to a chosen change in your own song.
```

**Check first:** Use a doorway or window to explain the transition; this source does not supply a reusable song.

## A stage reveal on the beat

[![An older performer standing on a gold-lit talent-show stage — @Strength04_X](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2090399674129940480/img/zvDQqERbmMVkeaIT.jpg)](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2090399966988550435)

**[@Strength04_X](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2090399966988550435) · Seedance 2.5** · creator-reported model<br>
[☞ Original post, video + prompt](https://x.com/Strength04_X/status/2090399966988550435) · Structured prompt in the post; the author credits @techhalla for the prompt idea.

**What to borrow:** Reserve a short pause before the musical change, then cut between performance and reaction.

Short source excerpt: “Silent focused stare then sudden beat drop”

**Original practice prompt (untested):**

```text
An adult percussionist waits on a small stage under warm light. Hold a calm medium shot for four seconds. On the first strong drum hit, cut to a closer view as the performer strikes one drum; switch the stage wash to blue. Finish on a wide view with a still ending. Time the cut and lighting change in the editor.
```

**Check first:** The comic stunt is not needed for the lesson. Start with a simple performable movement.
