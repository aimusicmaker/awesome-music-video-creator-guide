## Washed Out — The Hardest Part (2024)

[Full music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Nb-M1GAOX8) · [Official label release and director statement](https://www.subpop.com/news/2024/05/02/washed_outs_notes_from_a_quiet_life_available_june_28th) · [Paul Trillo’s artist statement](https://ars.electronica.art/hope/en/washed-out-the-hardest-part/) · [ISEA screening record](https://www.isea-symposium-archives.org/art-events/washed-out-the-hardest-part-by-paul-trillo/)

Paul Trillo directed and edited this officially released music video using Sora-generated visuals. The artist statement describes a journey through a couple’s life. AI identifies the visual method here; the song is Washed Out’s work.

**Watch for:** how forward movement connects scenes. Do changing details feel like memory, dreams, or an interruption? Pick one transition and describe the idea you would borrow.

## OK Go — The One Moment (2016)

[Full music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QvW61K2s0tA) · [Director’s notes and credits](https://okgo.net/2016/11/23/background-notes-and-full-credits-for-the-one-moment-video/) · [Official behind-the-scenes lessons](https://okgosandbox.org/the-one-moment/)

Damian Kulash’s notes describe triggered practical events, high-speed photography, robotic camera movement and post-production. The action underlying roughly the first three quarters lasts 4.2 seconds; that is not the production schedule. Camera, art, practical effects, editing and visual-effects teams are credited.

**Watch for:** where a burst or splash falls in the music. Why does slow motion keep its rhythmic force? Borrow one clear musical cue before attempting complex machinery.
