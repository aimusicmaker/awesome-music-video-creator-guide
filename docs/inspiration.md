<a id="lofi-girl"></a>
## 1. Lofi Girl — a place worth returning to

[Official channel](https://www.youtube.com/@LofiGirl) · [YouTube’s feature on the channel](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/rise-lofi-girl/)

YouTube’s feature describes a listening community built around an always-on stream and recognizable illustrated avatar. Our takeaway: a music visual can establish a familiar place without telling a new story every few seconds.

**Watch for:** how much of the composition stays still; how small moving details preserve a sense of life; whether you can recognize the scene at thumbnail size.

**Your exercise:** build an empty rainy train compartment, using reflections instead of a studying character. Keep one fixed camera and two moving details. Make the last frame compatible with the first, then test two consecutive repeats.

**Start:** [Last train home](../prompts/04-lofi-loop.md). Borrow the idea of a recurring environment, not Lofi Girl’s character or room design.

<a id="ok-go"></a>
## 2. OK Go — The One Moment

[Official video page](https://okgo.net/wp/2016/11/23/the-one-moment-music-video/index.html) · [Director’s production notes](https://okgo.net/2016/11/23/background-notes-and-full-credits-for-the-one-moment-video/)

The director explains how a brief sequence of precisely triggered events was expanded through high-speed photography. Our takeaway: decide which musical event the image serves before arranging visual effects.

**Watch for:** the difference between the brief underlying action and its presentation; which events punctuate the song; how preparation makes timing feel deliberate.

**Your exercise:** choose one clear beat in your own track. Prepare two shots of the same original paper lantern, closed and illuminated. Put the cut at that beat. Add no other large reveal until this one works. This is an editing exercise, not a recreation of the production’s machinery.

**Start:** [Chorus color change](../prompts/11-drop-reveal.md).

## 3. Gorillaz — Cracker Island

[Official artist-channel video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S03T47hapAc) · [Warner Music Japan’s official video directory](https://wmg.jp/gorillaz/mv)

Use this official animated music video as a viewing reference for a band’s visual identity. The exercise below is our recommendation, not an account of how the video was produced.

**Watch for:** which character details survive changes in framing, setting, and lighting; whether silhouettes and colors make a figure recognizable before you see the face.

**Your exercise:** design a new paper-cut messenger with a blue raincoat, round satchel, and white shoes. Write those three features in every shot brief. Start with three shots in one location and only then introduce a second location.

**Start:** [Paper-cut story](../prompts/07-paper-story.md). Do not import Gorillaz characters, logos, or songs into the exercise.

## Apply a reference without copying it

Write down the principle in one sentence: “A familiar setting supports repeated listening,” or “One change lands on one musical event.” Replace the subject, palette, setting, and music. Then evaluate your own output against that principle. The objective is a usable music visual, not an imitation of someone else’s identity.
