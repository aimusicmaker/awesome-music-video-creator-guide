# From listening to a finished video: MusicMaker resources

[Back to the guide](../README.md#toolkit) · [简体中文](brand-resources.zh-CN.md)

Choose a musical direction, then build the artwork, shots and audio files around it. Free entry points let you try a small scene; full song generation, portrait singing and other model routes may require credits or payment. [Pricing](https://musicmaker.im/pricing/).

Verification scope: footer categories and URLs were collected; not every tool, sign-in flow or generation route was opened or tested. The featured free entry points were individually read; see the [verification record](brand-resources.json).

Public display is not permission to download, adapt or commercially reuse a work. See the starter kit for its license. The site’s Music Library is personal generation history, not a public free stock library.

## Finish one small piece first

| Missing ingredient | Free entry and first action | Page claims and boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Your own cover | [Free image generator](https://musicmaker.im/free-chatgpt-images-2-5/): describe a subject, setting and aspect ratio; optionally edit one authorized reference image | Advertised without registration or subscription; queues may apply. Model branding is the site's claim |
| One moving scene | [Free short video](https://musicmaker.im/free-short-music-video-generator/): paste one tutorial prompt, select a ratio and keep the most stable part of the result | Currently 5 seconds, 480p, advertised without sign-up; arrange your soundtrack in the editor |
| A worn-record texture | [Lo-fi conversion](https://musicmaker.im/lofi-song-maker/): upload your song, convert, preview and save a suitable version | Advertised as free without registration; conversion does not grant rights to the input song |
| An editor-compatible file | [MP3 to WAV](https://musicmaker.im/audio-converter/mp3-to-wav/): convert and import your audio; use the [tag editor](https://musicmaker.im/mp3-tag-editor-online/) for track metadata | Advertised as free; format conversion cannot recover audio detail lost to compression |

Need a new song? Continue to [song creation](https://musicmaker.im/ai-song-generator/). Need a person to sing it? Try the [music video workflow](https://musicmaker.im/ai-music-video-generator/). Check estimated usage before generating; these advanced routes may require credits or payment.
