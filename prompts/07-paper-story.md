# 07 · Paper-cut story

[All recipes](README.md) · [Home](../README.md) · [Editing help](../docs/editing.md)

**Level:** Intermediate · **Editing target:** 20 seconds · **Status:** original brief, not render-tested.

## Bring these inputs

An original character sheet, a simple four-part story, and a track.

**Starting route:** [Short Music Video Generator](https://musicmaker.im/free-short-music-video-generator/). Use only input modes and durations currently offered by the tool. Longer plans require separate clips and editing.

## Image direction

Use this to create or choose a reference image. For text-to-video, the motion prompt can be used without an image when the tool permits it.

```text
Paper-cut messenger with blue raincoat, round brown satchel and white shoes, flat layered paper texture, muted cream and blue palette, original design, no lettering.
```

## Copy the motion prompt

```text
The same paper-cut messenger in a blue raincoat, round brown satchel and white shoes walks slowly from left to right across a simple paper city. Side-on locked camera, consistent cut-paper layers, one character, no text or costume changes.
```

## Copy one prompt per shot

Use the same reference image or reference sheet when supported. Each block below is a complete prompt for **one separate generation**. Timings describe the edit, not the generation request; generate a supported length and trim it.

<details>
<summary>0–5s · Approach</summary>

```text
One original paper-cut messenger in a blue raincoat, round brown satchel and white shoes walks left to right toward a cream paper doorway. Side-on fixed camera, blue-and-cream paper city, no text, no other people.
```

</details>

<details>
<summary>5–10s · Stop</summary>

```text
The same paper-cut messenger in a blue raincoat, round brown satchel and white shoes stands just left of the same cream doorway, facing right. One small settling movement, then stillness. Fixed side-on camera, no parcel handover, no text.
```

</details>

<details>

```text
Close view of a small plain coral paper parcel already resting on the doorstep of the same cream doorway in the blue-and-cream paper city. Subtle paper-shadow movement only. No hands, people, writing or extra parcels.
```

</details>

<details>
<summary>15–20s · Departure</summary>

```text
The same paper-cut messenger in a blue raincoat, round brown satchel and white shoes walks away toward the right, leaving the small coral parcel at the cream doorway. Fixed side-on camera, same layered paper texture, no extra people or text.
```

</details>

## Music direction

For an original song or instrumental; skip this if you already have audio.

```text
Playful instrumental: plucked strings, light woodblock, warm bass and a small recurring melody. Let the arrangement rise during the delivery and soften at the end.
```

## Assemble the edit

Create four separate shots: approach, stop at door, parcel resting on doorstep, departure. Use five seconds per shot, or trim supported output to fit. Avoid a difficult hand-to-hand transfer in the first version.

## If it fails

If the satchel changes size, reuse the character reference and reduce camera-angle changes. If hands fail, cut from the closed door to the parcel already placed.

**Before you export:** Raincoat, bag and shoes remain the same; the viewer can explain the story in one sentence.

Change one variable per retry and keep your best take. Use the [brief template](../starter-kit/brief-template.md) to record the actual inputs, output settings, and changes.
